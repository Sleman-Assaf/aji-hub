New document library

Name it, decide who can open it, then fill it. You can change any of this afterwards — except that nothing is uploaded until the rules are set.

1 What it is

The name and subtitle are what colleagues see on the tile in Policies & Manuals.

2 Who can open it

Chosen people only means the library is not shown to anybody else at all — not greyed out, not listed as locked. It simply is not there. By request means everyone can see it exists and ask you for it, and the people you name below are already in without asking.

3 Who helps you run it

Optional, and worth doing. They can add documents and folders and answer access requests alongside you — a library whose only owner is on leave can neither take a new document nor answer anybody asking for one.

{% include 'exchange/_people_picker.html' with search_url=people_url field='managers' preset=none placeholder='Search a colleague to add…' note='They keep whatever read access they already had — being a deputy is about running the library, not seeing it.' %}

4 Fill it

Drop in a zip and its folders are recreated exactly as they are inside it — files and all. Or leave this empty and add folders and documents once the library exists.
